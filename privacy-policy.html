Privacy Policy

How website and appointment inquiry information may be used.

Effective October 28, 2026 · Recovery and Wellness Massage · West Jordan, Utah

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Recovery and Wellness Massage website and inquiries submitted through it. It describes the categories of information the website may collect and the purposes for which that information may be processed. Specific third-party form and messaging configurations must be confirmed by the business.

Information you may provide

When requesting an appointment or responding to an offer, you may provide information such as your name, contact details, preferred appointment times, and anything you voluntarily include in your message. Please do not submit detailed medical histories or other sensitive health information in an open website form unless the practice specifically requests it through an appropriate secure process.

Website and embedded services

The website uses an embedded GoHighLevel/LeadConnector form for appointment inquiries and a promotional form on the homepage. It also embeds a YouTube-hosted video and loads Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser/device details, and form interaction information, under their own terms and privacy notices. The site may be hosted by a separate web hosting provider. Additional analytics or advertising trackers should be disclosed here if enabled.

Why information is used

Submitted information may be used to review appointment requests, respond to questions, discuss availability, provide requested services, administer promotional offers and maintain business records. Marketing communications should be sent only where the appropriate permissions and legal basis exist.

Sharing and service providers

Information submitted through forms may be handled by the practice and providers needed to operate those forms, process communications, or support services. We do not state that personal information is sold. Any additional business practices involving disclosure or sale/sharing must be evaluated and added if applicable.

Retention and security

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the request, business operations, or applicable obligations. No online system can guarantee complete security. Specific retention periods and safeguards should be confirmed by the business.

Choices and requests

You may contact the practice at the number below to ask about information you submitted, request a correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Privacy rights depend on applicable law. To stop text messages, follow the opt-out instructions in the SMS & Opt-In Policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, providers, or practices change. The revised date should be updated accordingly.
