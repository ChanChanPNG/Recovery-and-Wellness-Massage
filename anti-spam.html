Anti-Spam Compliance

Standards for responsible email and text communications.

Effective October 28, 2026 · Recovery and Wellness Massage · West Jordan, Utah

Our communication standards

Recovery and Wellness Massage should send marketing communications only when permitted, identify the sender clearly, avoid deceptive subject lines or misleading offers, and respect communication preferences and applicable law.

Marketing email

Commercial email should include accurate sender information, a working unsubscribe mechanism, and a valid physical postal address. The appropriate postal address and campaign footer must be confirmed before sending marketing emails. An appointment-related email may be subject to different rules depending on its actual content and purpose.

Marketing text messages

Promotional SMS requires the applicable consent; a customer inquiry alone does not establish that permission. Relevant messages should identify the business and provide effective opt-out options. See the SMS & Opt-In Policy.

Unsubscribing and complaints

Recipients may use the unsubscribe instructions in an email or respond STOP to eligible SMS messages. Other clear opt-out requests should also be respected. Questions or complaints can be raised using the contact number below.

Third-party platforms

Website forms and communications may be powered by GoHighLevel/LeadConnector or other providers. The business is responsible for verifying its actual campaign configuration, permissions, unsubscribe workflows, and suppression lists.

Review and updates

These standards should be reviewed whenever new advertising campaigns, messaging tools, or contact collection methods are introduced.
