SMS & Opt-In Policy

How text-message permissions and opt-outs should work.

Effective October 28, 2026 · Recovery and Wellness Massage · West Jordan, Utah

Messaging program

If Recovery and Wellness Massage offers an SMS program, messages may include replies to inquiries, scheduling communications, appointment reminders, and—only with the appropriate separate marketing permission—offers and promotions. Actual message categories, sending number, enrollment method, and automation settings must be confirmed in the business messaging platform before enabling campaigns.

Consent

Providing a phone number or submitting an appointment request does not automatically authorize promotional text messages. Any SMS enrollment mechanism should clearly identify Recovery and Wellness Massage as the sender, describe the relevant message types, and obtain the level of consent required for those messages. Marketing consent must not be a condition of purchasing massage services.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency may vary based on the messages requested and the program selected. Message and data rates may apply. The business must confirm whether and how these disclosures are presented at the actual point of opt-in.

How to stop messages

For compatible text programs, reply STOP to unsubscribe. Requests to revoke consent through other reasonable channels should also be honored. For help, reply HELP where supported or contact the practice at the number below. Confirm that STOP/HELP automation is configured and functioning before sending texts.

Privacy and information sharing

Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are used to administer messaging and may be processed by messaging or CRM service providers. Mobile opt-in and consent data should not be shared with third parties for their own promotional marketing. Consult our Privacy Policy for broader information handling details.

Consent records

The business should maintain records of the exact consent language, source, date/time, and permission status, and honor withdrawals of consent. These operational safeguards are requirements to verify, not a claim that current embedded forms implement them.

Contact

Questions about a message or opt-out may be directed to the practice at the contact number below.
